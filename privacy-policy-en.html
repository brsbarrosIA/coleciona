Privacy Policy — Coleciona

Last updated: September 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Coleciona application handles information when you use the application.

Coleciona is a collection management application that allows you to create albums, add sections and stickers, and track the status of your collection.

In summary: Coleciona does not require you to create an account, log in, or register personal information to use the application. Your collection data is stored locally on your device. The application uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display advertisements and may process certain device and usage data for this purpose, as described in this policy.

1. Who we are

The Coleciona application is developed by BRS Barros.

For questions, requests, or privacy-related matters, please contact us by email:

brsbsapps@gmail.com

2. Information stored by Coleciona

Coleciona was designed to work without requiring a user account.

Information you enter directly into the application, such as album names, sections, sticker numbers, and their respective quantities, is stored locally on your own device.

This information is used exclusively to provide the application's features, such as:

  • creating and managing collections;
  • tracking the stickers you own;
  • tracking missing stickers;
  • tracking duplicate stickers;
  • calculating collection progress;
  • application preferences and settings.

Coleciona does not send your collection data to its own server and does not maintain an online account associated with you.

3. Data stored locally

The application uses local storage on the device to maintain the data required for collection functionality and certain application preferences.

This data remains on the device and may be removed through the device's own management tools, including uninstalling the application or clearing the application's data.

Since Coleciona does not have an online account, there is no Coleciona database containing your collections that can be accessed through a login.

4. Advertising

Coleciona uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display advertisements within the application.

The advertising service is provided by Google and may collect and process certain device information and information related to application usage to provide, measure, and protect advertising services.

Depending on the service configuration and applicable conditions, this information may include:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate the approximate location of the device;
  • information about interactions with the application, such as opening the application and interacting with certain elements;
  • diagnostic and performance information, such as information related to startup time, performance, and stability;
  • device or account identifiers, as applicable, including identifiers used by advertising services.

This information may be used by Google for purposes such as advertising, analytics, performance measurement, and fraud and abuse prevention.

Google states that data processed by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit.

More information about how Google handles data related to Google Mobile Ads can be found in Google's official documentation.

Google Privacy Policy

5. Non-personalized ads

Coleciona is configured to request non-personalized advertisements.

Non-personalized ads do not use personalization based on browsing history or user behavior in the same way as personalized ads.

Nevertheless, the operation of advertising services may involve certain technical data and information necessary to display, measure, and protect advertisements, as described in this policy and in Google's policies.

6. Consent for advertising

When applicable, Coleciona uses the consent mechanism provided by Google to present options related to the use of data for advertising.

The availability of consent options may vary depending on the user's location and applicable legal requirements.

The application does not directly request personal information to create an account or to use your collections.

7. Information sharing

Coleciona does not sell your collection data and does not share the data you enter into your collections with third parties for its own purposes.

However, the application uses third-party services, such as Google Mobile Ads (AdMob), which may collect and process certain device and usage information as necessary to provide their services.

Processing carried out by these third-party services is subject to the respective privacy policies and terms of those providers.

8. Security

Coleciona adopts reasonable measures to reduce risks related to the processing of information used by the application.

Collection data is stored locally on the device instead of being sent to a Coleciona server.

For advertising services provided by Google Mobile Ads, Google states that data is encrypted in transit.

No electronic storage or transmission method can be considered absolutely secure. Therefore, although reasonable protection measures are adopted, absolute security against all risks cannot be guaranteed.

9. Data retention and deletion

Your collection data is kept locally on the device while it is necessary for the application's operation and while it remains stored on the device.

Since Coleciona does not have a user account or its own database associated with the user, there is no online Coleciona account that needs to be deleted.

Local data may be removed by the user through features available within the application or through the application's data management settings on the device's operating system.

Data that may be processed by third-party services, such as Google Mobile Ads, is subject to the retention policies of those respective services.

10. Sharing collections

Coleciona may offer features that allow information to be shared through the sharing functions available in the operating system.

When you choose to share information, the action is initiated by the user and may involve third-party applications or services selected by you.

The processing of information by these third parties is subject to their respective privacy policies.

11. Children

Coleciona does not directly request personal information from children for account creation or registration.

However, the application may display advertisements through Google Mobile Ads. Advertising and related data processing are subject to Google's applicable rules and settings and to relevant data protection laws.

12. Third-party services

Coleciona uses third-party services and libraries necessary for certain application features.

The main services relevant to privacy are:

  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) — used to display and operate advertising within the application.

These services may process information according to their own policies and terms.

13. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically to reflect changes to the application, the services used, data processing practices, or legal requirements.

When a material change occurs, the update date shown at the beginning of this page will be modified.

We recommend checking this page periodically for any changes.

14. Contact

If you have questions, comments, or requests related to this Privacy Policy or the processing of data by Coleciona, please contact:

BRS Barros
Email: brsbsapps@gmail.com

15. Applicable law

This Privacy Policy shall be interpreted in accordance with the applicable data protection laws and regulations governing the operation of Coleciona and its users.
